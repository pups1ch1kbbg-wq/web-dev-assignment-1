Pedro Pascal

1975 – Present | Acclaimed Chilean-American Actor

About Pedro Pascal

Pedro Pascal is a Chilean-American actor born in Santiago in 1975. After working for years in theater and taking television guest roles, he achieved international stardom through compelling performances in major franchise series. Renowned for his charisma and versatility, he has become one of the most recognizable and beloved actors in modern pop culture.

Important Career Milestones

  1. 1975: Born in Santiago, Chile, later moving to the United States.
  2. 2014: Gained widespread fame portraying Oberyn Martell in Game of Thrones.
  3. 2015-2017: Starred as DEA agent Javier Peña in the Netflix crime series Narcos.
  4. 2019-Present: Took on the iconic titular role in the Star Wars series The Mandalorian.
  5. 2023: Starred as Joel Miller in the critically acclaimed HBO adaptation of The Last of Us.

Key Roles & Contributions

  • The Mandalorian / Din Djarin in The Mandalorian
  • Joel Miller in The Last of Us
  • Javier Peña in Narcos
  • Oberyn Martell in Game of Thrones
  • Maxwell Lord in Wonder Woman 1984
